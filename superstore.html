What the dashboard shows

Four findings from the numbers on the pages above. The report explains each one in detail.

  • 2.5%

    Furniture keeps very little of what it sells. Technology and Office Supplies earn about 17 cents of profit per dollar of sales.

  • -$135K

    Discounts above 20% lose money. Orders sold at 0% to 20% off earned $422K in profit. Every band above 20% lost money.

  • -$25.7K

    Texas is the worst state. Ten of 49 states lose money, while California and New York together make about 52% of all profit.

  • 7.9%

    Central is the weakest region. It has the lowest margin and the highest average discount, 24%, against 11% in the West.
